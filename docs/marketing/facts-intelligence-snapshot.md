## Beacon attack angles (honest)

**Do not invent** Beacon aid/collections depth. Prefer:

1. **“Technology with Heart” is marketing; the ticker is Nelnet.**  
   Heart language ≠ ministry accountability.

2. **Unified Intelligence = unified lock-in.**  
   Admissions + Engagement + Financial + SIS under one vendor makes “leave” feel like moving the building.

3. **Engagement Intelligence is where Christian schools still bleed.**  
   Family App / alerts / portals don’t prove a message landed or capture parent replies into a school record. That’s Family Desk + Dinner Table Digest.

4. **Phased stewardship still wins.**  
   Keep Financial Intelligence for a season if needed; fix the family conversation layer now.

5. **Mission copy on their site proves the point.**  
   They literally sell to “Christian and Faith-Based Schools” with “Operational Clarity. Mission Focus.” — selling *into* mission is not *being* mission.
